Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What documents are required for court marriage?

For court marriage in Nepal, you need: Citizenship certificates of both parties, passport-size photographs, age verification documents, and in case of foreign nationals, passport and visa documents. We assist you with all documentation requirements.

How long does the court marriage process take?

The court marriage process typically takes 7-15 days from the date of application, depending on document verification and court schedule. We ensure the fastest possible processing.

What is the difference between mutual and contested divorce?

Mutual divorce is when both parties agree to divorce and its terms. It's faster and less complicated. Contested divorce is when one party disagrees, requiring court intervention and legal proceedings.

How much do your services cost?

Our fees vary depending on the service and case complexity. We offer competitive rates and transparent pricing. Contact us for a free consultation and detailed quote for your specific needs.

Is the process confidential?

Yes, we maintain strict confidentiality for all our clients. Your personal information and case details are protected under attorney-client privilege and handled with utmost discretion.
